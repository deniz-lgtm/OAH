Our Mission

Protecting affordability before it disappears.

We are a mission-driven housing operator dedicated to preserving naturally occurring affordable housing (NOAH) throughout Oakland. In a market where rising rents continue to displace long-time residents, we believe that protecting existing affordable units is one of the most effective ways to maintain community stability.

Our approach is simple: acquire well-located buildings, invest in their upkeep, and keep them affordable. We measure success not just in occupancy, but in the well-being of our residents and the strength of the neighborhoods we serve.

Our Commitment

Built on four principles.

A

Affordability

We are dedicated to maintaining rents that working families and individuals in Oakland can sustain over the long term.

Q

Quality

Affordable should never mean substandard. We invest in ongoing maintenance, safety, and improvements so our residents are proud to call our buildings home.

C

Community

We work alongside residents, neighbors, and the City of Oakland to support stable, healthy, and inclusive neighborhoods.

S

Stewardship

We view ourselves as long-term stewards of these properties, not short-term investors. Preservation is our purpose.
